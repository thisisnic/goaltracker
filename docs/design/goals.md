# Goals

Design notes for the goals part of the app. Written from the owner's
description, in their terms. Anything not settled is listed under Open questions rather than
decided.

## Context

The app is a central place to plan work and time: choose what tasks to do,
track progress towards goals, and work through problems. This document covers
goals only.

## Goals

- A goal has a statement, a "why", and a level: year, quarter or month.
- Yearly goals are a flat list. No grouping by area or type.
- A quarterly goal usually belongs to a yearly goal, and a monthly goal usually
  belongs to a quarterly one. Either can instead sit under a different level,
  or under nothing.
- Goals are set once at the start of their period. Their shape is up to the
  owner each time.

## Measuring

- A goal can be numeric, such as "run 500 km this year", or yes/no, such as
  "finish the garden".
- Numeric goals have a running total the owner updates by hand, whenever.
  Every update is kept with its date, so the value over time can be looked
  back on.
- Non-numeric yearly goals, such as "become a confident open-water swimmer",
  have no measure of their own. They are judged by whether their
  quarterly goals get hit, and by feel.

## End of period

- At the end of a month or quarter, each goal is marked hit or missed, and the
  next batch is set. Nothing else is required.

## Tasks

- A task can be linked to a goal, or not. The link is loose and there to
  experiment with, not a rule.

## Open questions

Both of these came up as "I don't know" and are deliberately left open until
there are real goals in the app to learn from.

1. **Recording feel.** For non-numeric goals, is a marker like on-track or
   behind wanted, or does feel stay in the owner's head? The owner noted that feel tends to
   end up linked to the SMART goals anyway, so it may need nothing extra.
2. **SMART pieces inside non-SMART goals.** A target such as "swim 5 km in
   open water" was written inside the yearly goal's description rather than as its
   own goal. This is close to what quarterly goals already do. It only becomes a
   question if SMART pieces are wanted that are not tied to a quarter or month.
